About Me

I am Andrew Yin, a passionate, respectful, and creative student who is both shy and confident at the same time. I like to make people laugh by making big, funny speeches, but I am surprisingly less outgoing with people during everyday interactions. I enjoy learning all subjects (my least favorite question is "What is your favorite subject?").

Interests & Hobbies

  • LEGOs are my favorite passtime because of the infinite number of designs and builds possible. LEGO sets with fun play features and easter eggs are also very enjoyable.
  • I enjoy sketching and drawing, especially large, futuristic and glassy buildings. In fact, some of my sketches resemble Kinkaid's glassy buildings. I also enjoy experimenting with colors in the color theory and creating fun color combinations.
  • A good book, movie, and other sources of story-telling are always welcome. A new book I just discovered is Ghost Map by Steven Johnson, which I read at school. Zootopia 2 and Hoppers, as mentioned before, were also brilliant movies.

Favorite Things

My favorite company is Apple because of their amazing design choices and excellent software and hardware. Apple stores are also very enjoyable because of their minimalistic design and the ability to test out all of their products in-store.

My favorite movie is Wall-E because of its message about enjoying life and its visually stunning animation. Note: During the making of this website, I discovered 2 amazing new movies that are giving Wall-E some competition for my number 1 spot - Hoppers and Zootopia 2 were extremely engaging movies that had fun plots, characters, and action-packed scenes.

I enjoy talking with friends and family, especially conversations in which we get to learn more about each others interests. I also enjoy watching movies with my family, especially if the movie contains a good plot.

My favorite food is anything and everything that is edible. I always find something delicious to eat and it is so hard to pick favorites!

Achievements

I am proud of my achievements in debate. I have attended multiple tournaments and even reached semifinals in the HUDL City Championships. Debate has allowed me to view the world with a more open mind and consider multiple perspectives.

I am also proud of my achievements in Calculator Applications, which I started during 7th grade. Calculator Applications is a quick and intensive UIL event that is extremely enjoyable and exhilerating to complete. In 8th grade, I was able to attend the TMSCA state tournament for the event.

Art is also something that I have done for a long time, ever since first grade. Throughout the years, I have won multiple awards and learned multiple techniques for art-making.
